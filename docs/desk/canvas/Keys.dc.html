hex codes paint themselves

# is no longer a key — it's a colour

  • type # and 3, 6 or 8 hex digits — #fff, #64748B, #64748BCC
  • the moment it's a valid code, the text becomes a chip filled with that colour
  • the chip's text turns dark or light, whichever reads on it
  • click or tap a chip: copy it, nudge it lighter or darker, or swap in a colour the project already uses
  • agents get the plain code, and colours in their replies come back as chips too
  • anything else after a # stays plain text — no popup, nothing to dismiss
make the archive action #64748B, the undo text #FAFAFA and the blocked dot #EF4444

how chips pick their text

#0A0A0A #262626 #64748B #3B82F6 #10B981 #F59E0B #FAFAFA

and what stays text: #later, #12, #ggg

the same on the pc and the phone

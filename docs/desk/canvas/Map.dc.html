structure

cellar on the desktop is all of cellar, plus the desk

one app, three roles. at the desk it's the control center: chat, projects, commands, the phone on the side. away from it, the phone is the remote. the part that actually runs agents is a small daemon inside the desktop app; it keeps running in the tray when the window is closed.

desktop app

the control center

cellar's four tabs, the desk sections, full chat with the keys, and the phone attached on the side over adb.

desk daemon

the part that works

runs agents and command jobs as hidden processes, keeps the event log, serves previews, talks adb. no window needed.

phone app

the remote

the same cellar app with the desk tab where profile was: speak a task, answer approvals, look at previews and the pc screen.
